The challenge

Home-based care carries unique billing burdens

Medicare, Medicaid, and managed care payers each apply different rules to home and community-based services — and the documentation requirements are unforgiving.

📄

Medicare Documentation Requirements

Home health billing under PDGM requires precise coding, face-to-face documentation, and OASIS accuracy — errors anywhere in the chain trigger denials.

✅

Prior Auth for Home Services

Authorization for skilled nursing, PT, OT, and home health aide services must be in place before a visit occurs — and expiration tracking is a constant risk.

🔄

Hospice Billing Complexity

Hospice claims require precise election periods, level-of-care distinctions, and physician certifications — each a potential denial point.

🧑‍💼

Back-Office Staffing Gaps

Home health agencies typically have lean administrative teams. Eligibility, authorizations, and AR follow-up compete for the same limited hours.
