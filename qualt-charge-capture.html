The problem

Delayed charge capture costs practices more than they realize

Every hour between a procedure and its documentation is a window for missed charges, coding errors, and billing delays.

⏱️

Documentation Lag

Physicians documenting procedures hours after the fact are more likely to miss billable items, misremember modifiers, and leave revenue uncaptured.

📝

Paper Rounding Sheets

Handwritten charge sheets get lost, misread, and require manual re-entry — introducing errors at every step of the billing chain.

🔗

Disconnected Workflows

When charge capture is separate from billing, coder and biller have to reconcile two systems — creating bottlenecks and AR aging.

📉

Unbilled Procedures

High-volume surgical and procedural settings are especially vulnerable to unbilled encounters — each one a direct hit to the bottom line.
